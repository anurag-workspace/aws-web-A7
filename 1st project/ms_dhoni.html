About the Documentary

From a small-town boy working as a railway ticket collector in Kharagpur to the captain who led India to every major ICC trophy. This documentary explores the calm mindset, strategic brilliance, and unforgettable moments of Captain Cool.

Career Highlights

  • 2007: Won the inaugural ICC T20 World Cup
  • 2011: Lifted the ICC Cricket World Cup with the iconic final six
  • 2013: Won the ICC Champions Trophy
  • 535: Total International Matches Played
